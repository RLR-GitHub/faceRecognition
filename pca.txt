Let X = { x_1, x_2, ..., x_n } be a random vector of observations
(faces whose pixel values have been vectorized)

1) Compute the mean (mu)

mu = ( 1 / n ) * summation of (x_i) from i[1,n]

2) Compute the Covariance Matrix S

S = ( 1 / n ) * summation of (x_i - mu) *( x_i - mu )^( T) from i[1,n]
( T for transposed)

3) Compute the eigenvalues lambda_i and eigenvectors v_i of S

S * v_i = lambda_i * v_i for i[1,n]

4) Order the eigenvectors descending by their eigenvalye.

The k principal components are the eigenvectors corresponding to
the k largest eigenvalues.
